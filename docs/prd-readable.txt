1. Vision
SaleScout is more than a garage sale finder.
It is a social treasure hunting platform that helps users discover amazing local deals while creating a
community around finding, sharing, and competing for the best treasures.
Instead of simply showing garage sale listings, SaleScout becomes the operating system for weekend
treasure hunters.
Every Saturday morning people open SaleScout to:
Discover nearby sales
Plan their route
Hunt for deals
Share their finds
Compete with friends
Earn rewards
Our long-term goal is to become the "Waze + Pokmon GO + Instagram" for treasure hunting.
